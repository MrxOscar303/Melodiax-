Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Melodiax ("we", "our", "us") is a music streaming and playback web application. This page explains what information we collect from users, why we collect it, and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: username, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).
  • Google / Facebook sign-in: if you choose to sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from that provider to create or match your account. We do not receive your password from these providers.
  • Profile information: profile picture, banner, bio/status message, and any customization you choose to add to your account.
  • Content you create: playlists, uploaded songs (for accounts with upload access), chat messages sent to friends, and friend connections.
  • Usage data: which songs you play or download for offline listening, so we can show this back to you across your devices.
  • Cookies: a secure, HTTP-only cookie is used to keep you signed in. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use this information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To provide core features: playing music, building playlists, chatting with friends, and offline downloads.
  • To send you account-related emails (such as email verification), via our email provider, Brevo.
  • To store uploaded images (avatars, banners, playlist covers) via our media hosting provider, Cloudinary.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not use third-party advertising trackers or analytics that profile you across other websites.

Third-party services we use

  • Google / Facebook - only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Cloudinary - to host images you upload (avatars, banners, covers).
  • Brevo - to send transactional emails (such as verification emails).

Chat messages

Messages you send to friends within Melodiax are stored so that your conversation history is available when you return. They are only visible to you and the friend you're messaging.

Your choices

  • You can edit or remove your profile information at any time from your account settings.
  • You can delete individual playlists, downloads, or messages you've sent.
  • You can request account deletion by contacting us using the details below.

Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, we remove your profile data, playlists, and messages within a reasonable period.

Children's privacy

Melodiax is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of Melodiax after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out via the Contact form on Melodiax, or use the "About us" link in the app.
